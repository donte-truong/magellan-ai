## What the successful run shows

- **Depth:** 24 tier-1 nodes and 9 tier-2 nodes; 10 of 34 planned tasks finished before the document cap, with 15 tier-1 and 9 tier-2 tasks still pending. Fair scheduling gave every tier-1 branch a turn before deepening. Corroboration works: the BCM2712 edge has three claims from three sources (confidence 0.85).
- **Planner quality:** queries were specific and source-typed (BOM lists, teardowns, SEC Form SD, FCC filings, foundry queries, `site:raspberrypi.com`), and relation types rotated between upstream inputs, manufacturer or facility, and supplier.
- **Gates held:** 75 candidates were rejected (28 predicate or direction, 22 scope, 12 span not found, 10 entailment, 3 disconnected) and only 1 model output was unusable.

## Coherence problems, in priority order

1. **Accessories treated as parts.** An Indian accessory buying guide contributed most tier-1 rows: cases, HATs, cameras, GPS modules, starter kits, keyboards, cables are PART_OF the product with product scope. The spans are verbatim and the verifier passed them, so the failure is semantic: "accessory for" is not "part of". Needs an explicit exclusion in extraction and verification prompts (accessories, kits, bundles, and compatible add-ons are not components), plus a scope rule that a kit containing the product cannot be part of it.
2. **Wrong kinds.** "Raspberry Pi Case for Raspberry Pi 5" became a facility; "Raspberry Pi 5 8GB" became a component of a desktop kit. Kind guidance was tightened after the run; a verifier check on kind should follow.
3. **Duplicate entities.** Four case nodes (`Official Raspberry Pi 5 Case`, `Official Raspberry Pi Case for Raspberry Pi 5`, `official Raspberry Pi case`, `case`), two M.2 HAT+ pairs, `RP1` versus `RP1 I/O controller chip`, and `BCM2712` versus `Broadcom BCM2712`. Deterministic resolution behaved as designed (no wrong merges), but `entity.review_needed` never fired because bare-name variants do not carry identifiers. The review event needs a near-duplicate signal (shared part number, one label contained in another within the same kind) that flags without merging.
4. **Retail and blog sources.** No first-party page was read for the root task; the official documentation only appeared for the M.2 HAT+. Source-type preference from the planner is not yet applied when ranking search results.
5. **Manufacturing versus design.** "Engineered by Broadcom" became MANUFACTURES. The prompt already says designer is not manufacturer; the verifier should be asked explicitly whether the span states physical manufacture.

## Fixes made during the session

Bounded Retry-After handling for 429s; video and social hosts skipped in research search; a task continues past one unusable document instead of being abandoned; planner output room raised for reasoning models; `OPENROUTER_REASONING` setting to disable or bound hidden reasoning; kind guidance in the extraction prompt.
